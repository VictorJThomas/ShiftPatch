The idea

Two tiles. The white one is the shift; the amber one is the patch laid over a gap. No crosses, no pulse lines. The wordmark sets Shift light and Patch heavy.

Clear space: one tile width on every side. Min size: mark 16 px, lockup 96 px wide.

06

Icon style

24 px grid, 1.75 px stroke, round caps and joins, one color (currentColor). Shown at 20 px by default, 24 px in tab bars. Icons support text; they never replace it, except on labelled icon buttons.

07

Accessibility rules baked in

Focus, targets, controls

Focus ring: 3 px --c-focus, 2 px offset, always visible on keyboard focus (shown on the first button). Touch targets 44 px (--tap); compact 36 px only on pointer-fine desktop tables with 8 px gaps (WCAG 2.2 target size).

Never color alone

  • Status = icon + word + tint. Errors = icon + message + heavier border.
  • Toasts use role="status" (success) and role="alert" (error).
  • Overnight shifts say "→ Sat 07:00" in text plus an Overnight tag.
  • Respect prefers-reduced-motion; skeleton shimmer stops.
  • All times 24-hour, facility-local, tabular numerals.
